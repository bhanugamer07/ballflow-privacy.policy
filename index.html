Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle

Privacy Policy • Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle ("we," "our," or "the Game"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play our mobile game.

2. Information We Collect

  • Local Game Data: All puzzle progression, coin balances, level records, and unlocked tube/ball skins are stored locally on your device via standard local storage. We do not upload your gameplay records to external proprietary servers.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable technical information (device model, operating system version, performance metrics) may be processed automatically by integrated SDKs to diagnose crashes.
  • Personal Information: We do not require account creation and do not collect personal identifiers such as names, home addresses, or phone numbers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our game may integrate trusted third-party services to deliver game features and monetization:

  • Google Play Games Services: Powers cloud achievements, leaderboard standings, and XP badges.
  • Google AdMob: Serves standard banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

For more details on Google's data practices, visit the Google Privacy & Terms site.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because game data is stored locally on your device, clearing application data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove your saved progress and customizations.

5. Children's Privacy

Ball Flow is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
