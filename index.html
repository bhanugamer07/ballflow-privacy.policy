Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle

Privacy Policy • Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle ("Ball Flow", "we", "our", or "the Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use and play Ball Flow.

Ball Flow is designed primarily as an offline puzzle game. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Ball Flow stores certain game information locally on your device so that your progress can be remembered between sessions.

This may include:

  • Coin balance
  • Current and unlocked levels
  • Level completion and star progress
  • Unlocked themes or game customizations
  • Selected theme
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Daily puzzle and reward status
  • Other game settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded by Ball Flow to our own servers.

3. Internet Connection

The core gameplay of Ball Flow can be played without an internet connection.

An internet connection may be required for third-party advertising services, including advertisements provided through Google AdMob.

4. Advertising

Ball Flow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location information, and information related to advertising interactions, depending on the user's device settings, consent choices, applicable laws, and the configuration of the advertising service.

This information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising, where permitted.

We do not directly control the information collected and processed by Google through its advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles information from partner apps and websites, please visit:

Google Privacy & Terms

5. Personal Information

Ball Flow does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, contacts, photographs, or precise location in order to play the game.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly through the game.

6. Permissions

Ball Flow is designed to require only permissions that are necessary for the functionality of the application.

The game does not require access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location for normal gameplay.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress stored locally remains on your device until you delete the application data, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the game.

Because this game data is stored locally, uninstalling Ball Flow or clearing its application data may permanently delete your saved coins, levels, themes, settings, and other game progress.

Advertising-related information may be retained or processed by Google according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Ball Flow is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because Ball Flow uses third-party advertising services, advertising settings and applicable requirements for children may depend on the configuration of the application, Google Play settings, applicable laws, and Google's policies.

9. Third-Party Services

Ball Flow currently uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and ad measurement.

Ball Flow does not currently use Firebase, Google Play Games Services, a cloud leaderboard, or a user account system for its core game functionality.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the application and its local data handling secure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
