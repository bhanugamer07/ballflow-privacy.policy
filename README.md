# Privacy Policy for Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle

**Last Updated:** August 17, 2026

---

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle** ("we," "our," or "the Game"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles user data, permissions, and third-party services.

---

### 2. Information We Collect
* **Local Game Data:** We store your game progression, level unlocks, high scores, equipped skins/themes, and coin balances locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`). We do not upload or store personal gameplay data on external private servers.
* **Device & Technical Information:** Non-personally identifiable technical information (such as device model, OS version, and crash logs) may be automatically processed by third-party services to ensure app stability and performance.
* **Personal Data:** We do not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personal identification information such as your real name, physical address, or phone number.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The game may integrate trusted third-party service providers to facilitate advertising, analytics, and platform achievements:
* **Google Play Games Services:** Used to provide leaderboards, player XP, and game achievements.
* **Google AdMob / Mobile Ads:** May serve banner, interstitial, or rewarded video advertisements in accordance with Google Play developer policies.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

---

### 4. Data Retention and Security
All saved progress is stored directly on your local device. If you clear the app data or uninstall the application, your locally saved game progress and customizations may be permanently deleted.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy
**Ball Flow: Color Sort Puzzle** is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
